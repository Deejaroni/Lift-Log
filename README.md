# Lift Log — standalone app

A workout journal that installs on a phone like a regular app and works offline.
No accounts. Each person's data stays in their own browser on their own device.

- **iPhone:** open the link in **Safari** → Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in **Chrome** → ⋮ menu → **Install app** (or *Add to Home screen*).

After that it opens full-screen from the home screen and works without a connection.

## Important for users: backups

Data lives only in that phone's browser. It does **not** sync between devices, and it's erased if
the app is deleted from the home screen or the browser's website data is cleared.
Now and then, open **Settings** (gear, top right) → **Your data** → **Back up everything** and save the
file somewhere safe. It holds your sets, plan, body weight and settings. **Restore** brings it back, or
moves it to a new phone; sets are never duplicated. The app reminds you after two weeks without a backup.
**Export sets as CSV** (same place) is for opening your sets in a spreadsheet.

Tip: on iPhone, the home-screen app and Safari keep separate data — log from the home-screen icon.

## How to use it

1. On **Log**, search for an exercise by name, muscle or equipment (short names like RDL work), or tap
   **Browse by movement**. Pick a muscle group first if you like; it only sorts the list.
2. Enter the weight and reps. The form changes for cardio (minutes, distance) and bodyweight moves.
3. Tap **Log set**. The rest timer starts, and the form clears for the next exercise.
4. **Plan** holds your weekly program, **History** every past day, and **Progress** your lifts over time.
   Tap the chosen exercise to see how it's done.
